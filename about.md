# About

I'm Utsav Rai. I write backend systems in Node and TypeScript, mostly around
production AI — model routing, fallback chains, evaluation harnesses, and the
unglamorous plumbing that keeps them from falling over at 2 AM.

## What I actually work on

Day to day that means things like moving inference off a hosted API and onto
self-hosted open-weight models behind a gateway, building the A/B machinery to
prove the swap didn't degrade quality, and writing the eval harnesses that catch
it when it does. A lot of it is latency work: streaming versus non-streaming,
where the tail sits, which timeout is lying to you.

The rest is ordinary backend craft — schema changes, queues, caches, migrations,
and the interesting ways each of them fails at scale.

## Why this site exists

Most engineering writing shows you the finished thing. It skips the part where
someone believed something false for six hours.

That gap is the useful part. A tutorial tells you what to type; a mistake tells
you which of your assumptions is load-bearing and wrong. **Learn From Mistakes**
is one post per real thing I got wrong, with the wrong belief stated plainly,
the mechanism explained properly, and the rule I follow now.

I'd rather be the person who publishes the outage than the person who has a
clean blog and a quiet incident channel.
